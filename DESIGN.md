# Enginara: a story on paper

## Approved direction

Two connected chapters: Imagine on warm paper, then Build inside a precise software workspace. Open with an original Higgsfield animation of the word Enginara being sketched, shaded, and finished with orange accents. The reference is the pencil-drawing progression of the supplied studio intro. The exploded technical laptop drawing follows. The Build handoff retains the centered Enginara identity and “You imagine. We build. We manage.” from enginara.tech, then explains the services before the Custom build / Proven systems choice. Avoid theatrical lighting and metallic product renders. The user-approved word drawing loops softly; the assembly remains scroll-driven.

## Palette and typography

- Paper: `#ded9d1`, expressed as a relative OKLCH color in CSS. Neutral Three.js occluding surfaces use this color to blend into the page; shells and component washes use coordinated paper tones.
- Raised paper: `oklch(91% .014 80)` for the illustrative website and service section.
- Ink: `oklch(27% .012 75)` for primary text and buttons.
- Secondary ink: `oklch(43% .017 75)` for body copy and annotations.
- Graphite: `oklch(48% .02 75)` for softer headings and marks.
- Rules: secondary ink at 30% opacity. Drawn geometry uses `#726b63`.
- Signal orange: `oklch(62% .165 43)` for logo details, drawing marks, progress, and active states. Use `oklch(48% .145 43)` for orange text and buttons on paper, and `oklch(70% .16 48)` for legible orange accents on charcoal.
- Build workspace: warm charcoal `oklch(20% .009 75)`, paper-colored text, crisp application previews. A deliberate change from the drawing surface into software.
- Assembly washes: warm ivory shell `#e8e2d7`, graphite keyboard tray `#7b827f`, slate-blue system board `#a4b9bc`, sage trackpad `#b0beb3`, copper connections `#bc7148`, orange accent keys `#d28a59`. Preserve these colors beneath colored-pencil grain, fine diagonal hatching, and selective crosshatching. Lightly retraced graphite edges keep the mechanical silhouette precise without looking like a CAD export. The software diagram carries blue for automation and sage for care.
- Share a restrained paper-grain overlay across the opening and assembly. Grade the film and still toward the page's paper color. Pencil shading stays fixed to component coordinates; antialias and fade tiny strokes to avoid shimmer as components move.
- Sora for headings and the wordmark; Manrope for body copy and controls. Keep the existing sans-serif identity, with no display italic in the opening.

## Structure and motion

1. The word Enginara is drawn on paper across the opening frame. The short introduction and project link sit beneath the drawing. The silent eight-second film loops: draw, hold the completed final frame for five seconds, fade to paper over 550ms, then redraw. Pause/play controls stop the entire sequence, including its hold countdown. Offscreen, hidden-page, and reduced-motion states also stop the sequence. Reduced motion and failed media use the finished drawing.
2. The drawing unfolds into components. Discovery gives the idea a foundation.
3. Parts connect and settle. Keep the keyboard intact in Foundation and Connections so its keys remain below the lid. In Experience, the tray first moves clear of the lid; only then do the key rows lift, straighten over visible sockets, and seat in a staggered sequence. The completed tray then lowers vertically into its recess. Keep key legends attached to the cap transforms. The copy explains integration and interaction design.
4. The assembled outline opens. The Enginara mark appears inside its screen.
5. The screen fills the viewport. The Enginara rails assemble beside a large wordmark, followed by Imagine, Build, and Manage connected by an orange progress line. The wordmark repeats with a long readable hold. Scrolling introduces custom software, automation/AI, and managed operations; their website-building, workflow, and care demonstrations keep running independently while visible. Finish with Custom build and Proven systems and their interactive software previews.
6. Automation and ongoing care show working HTML interfaces with timed demonstration cycles. Website and CRM previews also advance automatically; interacting takes over and pauses their playback. Offer pause/play, stop offscreen and on hidden tabs, and present completed static states for reduced motion. Return to paper for the approach and invitation to start a project.

Use a shared scroll playhead for geometry, annotations, copy, and screen projection. Allocate 54% of the 1400svh desktop journey to assembly and the remaining scroll distance to identity, services, and build choices. The opening film runs independently and never prevents scrolling. Component movement should explain assembly; no automatic rotation or mechanical loops. Keep the object large, with captions clear of its moving parts.

During the exploded assembly, all 15 component groups can be inspected: chassis and hinges, board, processor, copper connections, each cooling fan, each memory module, each battery cell, keyboard, trackpad, display, and fasteners. Clicking the model or choosing from the keyboard-accessible picker opens a front-facing close-up with the assembly faintly behind it and its Enginara service explanation on the left. Fit the geometry on the right. Hold the page and assembly pose while open; wheel or arrow keys rotate the part, and Front view resets it. Clicking empty paper, Escape, or Back to assembly closes it and restores picker focus and the same page position. Clicking the enlarged geometry keeps it open.

## Interface rules

- The opening extends the sketch language into the interface: lightly retraced button outlines, orange pencil hatching, drawn arrows, and an orange underline beneath “an idea.” Reuse the shared sketch controls for the hero and header. Keep body text and navigation crisp and readable.
- A drawn baseline and Imagine → Build → Manage trail connect the hero to the creation story. Fine construction marks stay secondary to the word drawing.
- Software interfaces retain precise controls, fine rules, restrained corners, and generous space.
- Use annotations when they explain a specific part; leaders must connect to that part's projected position.
- Website and workflow demonstrations are explicitly illustrative. Make controls useful and keep their palette warm, with orange for the active action.
- Desktop is the current design focus. Reduced motion and unavailable WebGL receive normal document flow and immediate access to the work.
- No glass cards, glow effects, fake live metrics, or identical icon-card service grids. Charcoal is reserved for the software chapter.
