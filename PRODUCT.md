# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Companies that need custom software, workflow automation, and ongoing technical operations support. Their teams want a capable partner who can translate business needs into dependable systems without making the work feel impersonal or opaque.

## Product Purpose

Ops & Code turns operational needs into custom software and automated workflows, then provides the technical support required to keep those systems useful, reliable, and evolving. Success means clients gain working systems and a long-term operational partner, not another disconnected tool.

## Brand Personality

Bold, poetic, and engineered. The brand should communicate technical confidence through a human, story-led sensibility, making technology feel considered rather than mechanical or transactional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gradients, robot or AI imagery, gears, circuit-board clichés, excessive visual references to artificial intelligence, and bland corporate-consulting identities. The brand should feel technical without becoming a collection of familiar tech symbols.

## Design Principles

- Make complexity feel coherent.
- Express automation as purposeful flow.
- Unite building software with supporting its operation.
- Tell a compelling story instead of pushing a product.
- Balance technical precision with human imagination.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. The identity must remain legible and recognizable without depending on differences between teal and orange, including in monochrome and for viewers with color-vision deficiencies.
